# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University students studying INT191, reading Thai explanations with English database terms on laptops and phones, in daytime classes and evening study sessions.

## Product Purpose

An independent interactive database textbook and laboratory. Help students understand relationships, explain normalization decisions, practice and prepare for exams without requiring login. Preserve the existing Astro and React learning tools.

## Brand Personality

Clear, curious, capable. Friendly Thai explanations, precise database terminology, immediate useful feedback.

## Anti-references

The inherited Knowledge website styling is not a design constraint. Avoid large decorative heroes, excessive gradients, generic identical card grids, distracting animation and tiny diagrams. References from the original brief: Brilliant for learning by doing, Khan Academy for independent study, developer documentation for navigation.

## Design Principles

- Keep the next learning action visible.
- Explain with records and relationships, not decoration.
- Give content more room than navigation.
- Separate progress, next actions and reference material clearly.
- Let students choose any topic independently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Thai and English, light and dark modes, keyboard operation and visible focus. WCAG AA contrast, semantic tables and diagram text alternatives. Color never carries meaning alone. Respect reduced motion; contain wide tables and diagrams inside their own scroll regions.
